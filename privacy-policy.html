Privacy Policy

YAHA TOUR AND TRAVELS
Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YAHA TOUR AND TRAVELS collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our app or booking website.

1. Information we collect

When you register, sign in or request a trip, we may collect your email address, account identifier, name, mobile number and booking details. Booking details can include vehicle type/model, trip type, pickup and drop locations, travel date, estimated distance, estimated fare and payment method selected.

Driver and administrator accounts may also have role and account-status information so the service can provide the relevant dashboard and booking functions.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, receive and manage bookings, calculate or confirm fares, assign drivers, show booking status, provide customer support and maintain the service.

3. Where information is stored

Account and booking information is stored using Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. These services are provided by Google. Their processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy information.

4. Sharing information

Booking information may be visible to the customer who made the booking, authorised administrators and the driver assigned to that booking, as needed to operate the service.

If you choose to open a WhatsApp booking or support link, information included in the message will be shared with WhatsApp when you proceed. WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy policy applies.

We do not currently use the app to process card payments. A payment method marked as online is subject to confirmation by the administrator.

5. Advertising

The current app version does not include an advertising SDK or display in-app advertisements. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that change is made available.

6. Data retention and deletion requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage accounts, bookings, customer support, security and applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction or deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business records.

7. Security

We use Firebase Authentication and database access rules to help protect account and booking information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

8. Children's privacy

This service is intended for people arranging travel services and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with its updated date.
